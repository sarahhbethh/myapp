AT&T Holiday Hub

AT&T × TPN

Role

Lead Designer

Timeline

2024 – 2025

Platform

Mobile Web (unlock.att.com)

Deliverables

Wireframes, Prototypes, UI Design, Bilingual Support (EN/ES)

Designed the AT&T Holiday Hub — a digital gift guide experience for in-store customers during the holiday season. The challenge was creating a clean, focused landing page that didn't overwhelm customers while still surfacing the most relevant content.

The client was initially unsure what needed to be prioritized on the homepage. Through strategic recommendations based on prior year performance data and multiple proof-of-concept prototypes, we established a streamlined three-entry homepage with dedicated pages for the Gift Guide, Expert Picks, and Holiday Arcade games.

I also led the design and continuation of the Holiday Arcade games from the previous year — interactive experiences that drove customer engagement and were brought back by client request for a second consecutive year.

Google Wallet Demo

AT&T × Google

Role

Designer

Timeline

December 2022 – January 2023

Platform

In-store digital display (1080×2400)

Deliverables

Storyboards, Motion Design, Video Production

Created an animated attract loop for Google Pixel demo devices in AT&T retail stores, promoting Google Wallet's features. The 20-second looping video showcases tap-to-pay functionality, boarding passes, concert tickets, and loyalty cards.

The project involved close collaboration with both AT&T and Google stakeholders through multiple design rounds, addressing feedback on animation timing, typography, logo placement, and brand compliance. A key challenge was coordinating the launch timing with a new Google phone release.

The final deliverable balances Google's brand guidelines with AT&T's retail environment, featuring clean typography, smooth transitions, and a call-to-action driving Google Play downloads.
